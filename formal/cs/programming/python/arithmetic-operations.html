Basic arithmetic


        # addition
        10 + 5
        # ans: 15
        # subtraction
        10 - 5
        # ans: 5
        # multiplication
        10 * 5
        # ans: 50

Division operations


        # division (always returns float)
        10 / 5
        # ans: 2.0
        10 / 3
        # ans: 3.3333...
        # floor division
        10 // 3
        # ans: 3

Floor division keeps the whole part: 10 / 3 is 3.33…, rounded down to 3.

Modulus


          # modulus (remainder)
          10 % 3
          # ans: 1
          7 % 3
          # ans: 1
          15 % 4
          # ans: 3

Make as many full groups as you can; the modulus is what is left over.

Exponentiation


          # power/exponentiation

Operator precedence


          # PEMDAS rules apply
          10 + 5 * 2
          # ans: 20 (multiply first)
          (10 + 5) * 2
          # ans: 30 (parentheses first)
          2 + 3 * 4
          # ans: 14
  1. 10 + 5 * 2
  2. 10 + 10
  3. 20
  1. (10 + 5) * 2
  2. 15 * 2
  3. 30
  1. 2 + 3 * 4
  2. 2 + 12
  3. 14

Remember

/ vs //

/ always gives a float; // rounds down, even for negatives (-10 // 3 is -4).

% is the remainder

What is left after making as many full groups as possible.

Precedence

Parentheses, **, then * / // %, then + -; equal ranks go left to right, except **.

No dividing by zero

5 / 0 raises ZeroDivisionError.
