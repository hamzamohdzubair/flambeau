### Hyping AI isn't new

* *There are now in the world machines that think, that learn, and that create* &mdash; **Herbert Simon** (1957)
* *Within ten years a digital computer will discover and prove an important new mathematical theorem* &mdash; **Allen Newell** (1958)
* *Within our lifetime machines may surpass us in general intelligence* &mdash; **Marvin Minksky** (1961)
* *Machines will be capable, within twenty years, of doing any work a man can do* &mdash; **Herbert Simon** (1965)
* *Within a generation...few compartments of intellect will remain outside the machine's realm—the problem of creating 'artificial intelligence' will be substantially solved* &mdash; **Marvin Minsky** (1967)
